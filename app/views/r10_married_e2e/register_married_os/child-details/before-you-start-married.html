Before you start

What you need to provide

A registrar will check this information after you submit the application.

You’ll be told when the registration is complete, or if there are any errors to correct.

Part 1: Child’s details

Including name, place and date of birth, and sex.

Part 2: Mother’s details.

Including name, place of birth, job role, and previous names.

Part 3: Father or parent’s details

Including name, place of birth, job role, and previous names.

Part 4: Questions for government services

For example, parents’ ages and employment status.

Child and parents’ details are recorded on the birth certificate of registration.

Saving your answers

You can save your progress if you need to stop and continue later.

For data protection, applications are deleted if you do not submit within 14 days.

Birth certificates

You can pay for birth certificates in advance. Each copy is £12.50. They’ll be sent when the register office confirms the registration.

Birth certificates are used to apply for passports, benefits and other services.

You can order copies online any time after the registration.
